Sweet Mother

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Sweet Mother is a health education mobile app developed to give mothers, pregnant women, and families practical information on maternal and child health. This app is built and maintained in connection with the Health Education and Empowerment Initiative (HEDEN). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

The short version: Sweet Mother does not require you to create an account, does not collect personal information such as your name or email address, and does not contain advertising or analytics tracking. The app stores your language preference on your own device and fetches publicly available health articles from our content server.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information stored on your device

The app saves the following data locally on your device only, so your settings persist between sessions:

  • Your selected app language (English, Igbo, Yoruba, Hausa, or Nigerian Pidgin)
  • Whether you have completed the app's introduction screens
  • A local cache of health articles, so content loads faster and works offline

This information is not transmitted to us and stays on your device unless you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

1.2 Information sent over the network

To display health articles, the app requests content from our content server at sweetmother.com.ng. These requests include standard technical information that any internet request includes (such as your device's IP address and request timestamp), which may be present in server logs for security and reliability purposes. We do not attach any personal identifiers to these requests, and no account or login is required to use the app.

1.3 Information we do not collect

  • We do not require account registration, sign-in, or passwords
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth
  • We do not use advertising networks or behavioral tracking SDKs
  • We do not use analytics services to track your activity in the app

2. App Permissions

PermissionWhy it's requested
Network access Required to download health articles and images from our content server.
Notifications Reserved for potential future updates (such as new content alerts). The app does not currently send push notifications.

3. Donations

Sweet Mother includes an optional "Donate" button that opens a donation page hosted by Flutterwave in your device's web browser. Any payment or personal information you choose to provide on that page is collected and processed directly by Flutterwave, not by the Sweet Mother app. We do not receive or store your payment details. Please review Flutterwave's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle your data.

4. Third-Party Links

The app may link out to external websites, such as our organization's website or video content hosted on third-party platforms (e.g. YouTube, for embedded videos). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any information.

5. Children's Privacy

Sweet Mother is intended for a general audience, including parents and expectant mothers, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal information from any user, this generally applies equally to users of all ages.

6. Data Retention and Your Choices

Since app preferences and cached content are stored only on your device, you can remove this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. As we do not hold personal data about you on our servers, there is no account data for us to delete on request.

7. Medical Disclaimer

Sweet Mother is intended for educational and informational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how the app handles data, please contact us at info@sweetmother.com.ng.

Sweet Mother is developed in connection with the Health Education and Empowerment Initiative (HEDEN).
